What it does

One app for the parts that pay

Pricing, clients, and cash flow, without a laptop, a login wall, or a monthly training session.

Estimate from a photo or your voice

Snap a bid or a scope, or just talk the job out loud. Homestead reads it into line items you can price. Nothing is invented; anything unclear gets flagged.

Price it two ways

Build from your costs with a markup, or analyze a bid you already have. See your margin and break-even before you ever send a number.

A private client portal

Share one link: progress, schedule, photos, and their balance. Your costs and margins are never on it, and only the client with the link can see it.

Approvals and text updates

Clients approve change orders with a tap and opt in to text updates. One approval state, whether they tap the portal or text you back.

Every job in one pipeline

Track stages, tasks, change orders, payments, and what you are still owed. Log real costs against the budget as the work happens.

Yours, and offline

Installs like an app, runs with no signal, and keeps your data on your device. Optional encrypted cloud sync keeps your phones in step.

How it works

Three steps, start to paid

1

Price the job

From your costs or a photo. Homestead shows the price, your margin, and the break-even so you know the floor before you bid.

2

Win it, share a portal

Send a branded proposal and a private progress page the client can actually follow, without ever seeing what the job costs you.

3

Track the work, get paid

Log costs against the budget, move the job through its stages, approve changes, and keep an eye on the balance due.

Client portal

Your clients see progress, never your margins

Every job can have a private link. The client watches the project move, sees photos and their balance, and approves changes with a tap. What they never see is your cost, your markup, or your margin.

  • Status, schedule, photos, and balance in one page
  • Tap to approve change orders, or approve by text
  • Costs and margins are structurally left out, not hidden
